the pipeline

Upload, teach, and let the graph do the rest.

Ingestion, live session, and analysis are three separate stages, each independently inspectable.

ingest

Slides become a teaching graph

Your material becomes the beats the Director expects the session to hit.

direct

Turn-controlled session

Personas never self-initiate speech. The Director gates every turn.

record

Full-fidelity capture

Audio, video, and transcript are captured together, timestamp-aligned.

analyze

Evidence-backed report

A LangGraph analysis pipeline scores the session and cites its source.

the report contract

Every score is a citation, not an opinion.

The analysis graph does not summarize a vibe. It extracts evidence, scores against a rubric, and refuses to emit a number it cannot point to in the recording. That is the same contract closewire uses for negotiation scoring, applied to a classroom instead of a deal room.
